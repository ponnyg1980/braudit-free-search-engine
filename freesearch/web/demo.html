Braudit sandbox

Every journey below runs end to end — searches are real, the wizard is real, checkout completes — but on the demo tenant: nothing reaches Zoho, Xero or Stripe, no card is charged and no email is sent. The striped banner stays on every screen so a demo can never be mistaken for live.
