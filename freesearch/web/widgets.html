Widgets 1–8 — unchanged numbering

1. Search box — Free Search variant

The compact entry point. Takes the name, mints the journey session, then hands the visitor to the full Free Search wizard on step 2 (logo & tagline).

Use it on: the home page, service pages, blog sidebars — anywhere you want the search to start without sending someone to another page first.

As it appears on your page

2. Search box — Quick Search variant

Same box, different journey: straight to classes, then results. Lands in Zoho as Lead Source Quick Search rather than Free Search, so the split test stays honest.

Use it on: paid landing pages and anywhere you want the shortest path to a result.

Helps someone work out which classes they need — from a competitor’s trademark, their own company, or by picking classes directly. Nothing here calls a model, so there is no email gate and no fair-use limit: reset, select classes, then email the list to yourself.

Use it on: guidance and “which class am I?” pages, where the question is classification rather than availability.

As it appears on your page
<script src="https://braudit-free-search.onrender.com/embed.js" data-widget="class-assistant" data-tenant="tmh" async></script>
Emailing the classes creates a Zoho lead with Lead Source Class Tools, deduplicated against existing contacts and leads.

9. Class assistant with AI tools New

The same assistant as #7 with the AI tools grouped in their own panel and labelled as such. Reading a website pre-fills the questionnaire rather than classifying straight off the page, and the questions are written for any trade — a plumber, a builder or a charity answers them as naturally as a product brand does.

Use it on: pages where you want the smarter suggestions and are happy to ask for an email address first.

As it appears on your page
<script src="https://braudit-free-search.onrender.com/embed.js" data-widget="class-assistant-ai" data-tenant="tmh" async></script>
Fair use. Five AI calls per email address, then the tools close and the person is flagged in Zoho as AI Tools Blocked. An adviser unticking that box hands back a full five on the next call. Anyone on the Safe Email list — every @thetrademarkhelpline.com address, plus introducers and resellers ticked as AI Safe Sender — is never counted and never blocked.

10. Class & Terms Builder — staff and resellers NewInternal

Name, classes, then review and send. No register search and no explaining what the business does: whoever is using it already knows the answer, so it goes straight to the full class list and the UKIPO terms underneath each one. The review screen is where terms are amended, and the closing question — anything not covered by the terms above? — writes free text into Additional Terms Requested.

Use it on: staff pages and reseller portals. Opened from a Zoho Deal it writes back to that deal’s Classes & Terms; opened cold it emails the list as a CSV and creates the lead.

What the client receives

The emailed search report

Every Free and Quick Search ends with an email, and this is the page it links to. It is not a widget — it has its own address, one per search, and it renders the complete stored result rather than the shortened version on the results screen. The button at the top prints it to PDF.

Live sample: a real Quick Search for Northstar Coffee in class 30, run on 22 August. One similar mark was flagged and none scored above low risk, so this is the encouraging end of the range — the same page carries a very different tone when the register is crowded.

As it arrives with the client
Open the full report in a new tab
https://www.thetrademarkhelpline.com/search-report/?fs=<session> https://braudit-free-search.onrender.com/report/<session> (same page, engine host)
The address is the journey session id, so the link in the email is unique to that search and keeps working afterwards — there is nothing to log in to.
