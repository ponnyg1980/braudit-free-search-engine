Embeddable widgets — test page

Every widget below is embedded the same way a partner site would do it: one script tag. They are live, so anything you type creates real journey records.

1. Search box — Free 2. Search box — Quick 3. Search box — bar style 4. Full wizard 5. Class assistant 6. Trademark / owner lookup

1. Search box — Free Search variant

The compact entry point. Takes the name, mints the journey session, then hands the visitor to the full Free Search wizard on step 2 (logo & tagline).

Use it on: the home page, service pages, blog sidebars — anywhere you want the search to start without sending someone to another page first.

As it appears on your page
<script src="https://braudit-free-search.onrender.com/embed.js" data-widget="search-box" data-variant="free" data-tenant="tmh" async></script>

2. Search box — Quick Search variant

Same box, different journey: straight to classes, then results. Lands in Zoho as Lead Source Quick Search rather than Free Search, so the split test stays honest.

Use it on: paid landing pages and anywhere you want the shortest path to a result.

As it appears on your page
<script src="https://braudit-free-search.onrender.com/embed.js" data-widget="search-box" data-variant="quick" data-tenant="tmh" async></script>

3. Search box — bar style

The same widget in a single-line treatment: no heading, no supporting copy, just the field and the button.

Use it on: headers, footers, sticky bars — places where a full panel would be too heavy.

As it appears on your page
<script src="https://braudit-free-search.onrender.com/embed.js" data-widget="search-box" data-variant="free" data-style="bar" data-tenant="tmh" async></script>

4. Full search wizard, embedded

The entire Free Search journey inside your page — name, logo and tagline, territories, classes and terms, results. The widget reports its own height, so the page grows with it and the footer never jumps.

Use it on: your Free Search page itself, or a partner’s site that wants the whole tool rather than a link to it.

As it appears on your page
<script src="https://braudit-free-search.onrender.com/embed.js" data-widget="free-search" data-tenant="tmh" async></script>
This one is tall by design. Swap data-widget for brand-audit to embed the audit form the same way.

5. Class assistant

Helps someone work out which classes they need — from a competitor’s trademark, their own company, or a description of what they sell.

Use it on: guidance and “which class am I?” pages, where the question is classification rather than availability.

As it appears on your page
<script src="https://braudit-free-search.onrender.com/embed.js" data-widget="class-assistant" data-tenant="tmh" async></script>

6. Trademark / owner lookup bar

A register lookup with three purposes, switchable at the top of the widget: pull classes and terms from a competitor’s mark, check a trademark’s status, or find the IDs behind a portfolio.

Use it on: internal tools and staff pages first — it exposes register detail rather than selling a search.

As it appears on your page
<script src="https://braudit-free-search.onrender.com/embed.js" data-widget="search-bar" data-tenant="tmh" async></script>
