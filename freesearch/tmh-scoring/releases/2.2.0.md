# Release record — tmh-scoring 2.2.0 (19 Sep 2026)

**Change:** finding G ruled. Legal forms are now stripped per the record's own
jurisdiction, from a generic list plus a per-jurisdiction list, at Refine
rather than inside scoring. **Intended behaviour change: yes, and measured
below.** The scoring functions themselves are untouched.

## The ruling

Neither existing pattern was adopted. Both failed the same way — applying a
legal form outside the country whose law defines it:

    audit  stripped UK, GROUP, HOLDINGS   "UK & FRIED CHICKEN" -> "& FRIED CHICKEN"
    Watch  stripped a bare AS and SA      "SHOP AS YOU GO LIMITED" -> "SHOP YOU GO"

AS is a legal form in Norway and a preposition in England, so whether to strip
it is a property of the RECORD, not of the word. `LEGAL_FORMS_GENERIC` holds
the forms that mean the same thing anywhere; `LEGAL_FORMS_BY_JURISDICTION`
holds the rest and is consulted only when the record's jurisdiction matches.
`DESCRIPTIVE_SUFFIXES` (HOLDINGS, GROUP, INTERNATIONAL, GLOBAL, UK) is a
separate list, **off unless a caller asks**, because those words are usually
the brand rather than the legal form.

Per-jurisdiction lists start short deliberately. They grow when a real record
shows a form that should have been stripped and was not — a fact — rather
than from a guess about what a country might use, which is an assumption that
quietly moves scores.

## Measured impact

| | names tested | normalise differently | bands that move |
|---|---|---|---|
| Audit company rows on record | 2,064 | **167 (8.1%)** | **2** |
| Distinct names, Watch ledger — audit pattern | 1,183 | 26 (2.2%) | — |
| Distinct names, Watch ledger — Watch pattern | 1,183 | 3 (0.3%) | — |

The two products now produce the **same** normalisation for all 1,183 names.
They are no longer two lists that happen to agree; they are one function.

Both bands that move are instructive, and the second is the argument for
keeping the descriptive list available rather than deleting it:

    CLEDOP UK LIMITED
      before  'CLEDOP'                  Low
      after   'CLEDOP UK'               Low/Medium     (UK is part of the name)

    SPECIALIST SECURITY GROUP LIMITED
      before  'SPECIALIST SECURITY'     Low/Medium
      after   'SPECIALIST SECURITY GROUP' Low          (GROUP no longer stripped)

`tools/band_diff.py` on 55,526 Watch records and the audit run: **ZERO
DIFFERENCES**. That is expected and is the point — band_diff replays the
scoring functions, and this change is upstream of them. It confirms the
scorer was not disturbed; the table above is the measurement that matters.

## Refine, not Score

`normalise_company_name()` returns **both** the compared form and the list of
what was stripped, and the audit company row now carries `compared_as` and
`legal_forms_stripped`. The client's report still shows "UK Health Radio Ltd";
the Settings panel can show "compared as: UK Health Radio". A caller that
keeps only the first half has recreated the defect this release fixes: until
now the normalised name existed for the instant the scorer needed it and was
never seen again, which is how two different lists ran side by side for weeks
without anyone noticing.

## Migration

`LEGAL_FORMS_AUDIT` and `LEGAL_FORMS_WATCH` are kept for one release, unused
by either product, so the ruling can be read against what it replaced. Remove
in 2.3.0.
